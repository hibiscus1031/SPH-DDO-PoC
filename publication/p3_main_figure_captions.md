## Figure 1. Fixed-time defect formulation and pre-learning qualification framework

**(a)** The spatial discretization defect is the sampled analytical continuum operator minus the corresponding low-cost SPH spatial operator at one fixed time; time-integration, next-state, and rollout errors are excluded. **(b)** Analytical quantities construct and audit the target, while only reference-free deployment-compatible fields enter the observable path. The dashed separation denotes the audited information firewall. **(c)** The qualification hierarchy separates signal resolvability (H1), controlled scaling (H2), operational observable identifiability (H3), locality (H4), representation learning (H5), and generalization (H6). **(d)** None of the three primary dynamic mappings satisfied all fresh H3 criteria. Locality was therefore not interpreted, and no neural stage was initiated. H4–H6 are not depicted as failed experiments. The framework concerns a fixed-time route and does not assert classical structural identifiability or universal unlearnability.

## Figure 2. Signal resolvability and componentwise scaling

**(a)** Frozen component ratios (R_c=T_c/U_c) and stratified-bootstrap lower bounds (L_{95,c}) are shown on a logarithmic axis with limits (R_c\geq10) and (L_{95,c}>5). (R_c) measures separation of defect magnitude from qualified numerical/reference uncertainty; it is not a physical signal-to-noise ratio. **(b)** Qualification matrix for refinement and spectral tracks in regular and 5% controlled-disorder layouts at the canonical support ratio (h/\Delta x=4). Letter codes retain the decision in grayscale. **(c)** Normalized density-rate defect along the V100 refinement track for all three matched replicates in regular and disorder layouts. The lines display frozen responses and do not define fitted convergence orders. **(d)** Frozen refinement monotonicity fractions for the momentum components and derived total acceleration; the horizontal line marks the 0.75 requirement. Density rate satisfied both regular and tested-disorder scopes, whereas pressure, viscosity, and total acceleration were qualified only in the regular scope. Interpolation density did not satisfy the scaling criteria despite satisfying H1.

## Figure 3. Mechanism-stratified development evidence and initial identifiability design

**(a)** The development atlas contains 512 complete cases, balanced as 128 cases in each scientific family: single-mode, multimode, directional/mechanism, and controlled disorder. **(b)** Five folds are separated by target-free field lineage; a held-out fold supplies queries, and the remaining four folds supply permitted neighbours and diagnostic-oracle fits. Particles are first reduced within case, with equal case and fold weighting. **(c)** Operational identifiability compares defects among nearby deployment-observable feature vectors through nearest-neighbour disagreement, conditional target variance, and fixed non-neural diagnostic oracles; feature-space coverage is tested separately. **(d)** Initial C3/L3 all-gates summary for the three primary dynamic components. P denotes a satisfied criterion and N a criterion not satisfied. The matrix retains favorable medians, conditional-variance results, oracle results, and coverage alongside adverse tail and family evidence. The 65,536 formal samples comprise exactly 128 target-blind particles from each development case. This is development evidence, not held-out generalization evidence.

## Figure 4. Initial operational-identifiability outcome and mechanism diagnosis

All panels use the frozen 512-case development evidence. **(a)** Initial nearest-neighbour disagreement medians and 90th percentiles for the C3/L3 representation. The dashed and dotted lines mark the median limit of 0.25 and P90 limit of 0.60, respectively; favorable medians remain visible. **(b)** Conditional-variance upper 95% bounds and best fixed-oracle normalized root-mean-square errors are divided by their frozen upper limits (0.35 and 0.50) solely for common-axis visualization; bar labels give the untransformed frozen values. **(c)** Field-family NRMSE for each component's preregistered best diagnostic oracle. The frozen family limit is 0.75. **(d)** Matched controlled-disorder ablation comparing geometry-only observables (C0) with geometry plus low-cost consistency residuals (C1) at L1. Conditional-variance values are divided by 0.25 only for display. Simple consistency augmentation did not provide a uniform pressure or viscosity rescue. These diagnostic oracles and ablations do not constitute model selection or a renewed H3 test.

## Figure 5. Failure attribution, prospective redesign, and evidence separation

**(a)** The 512-case development atlas became consumed observable-design evidence after initial diagnosis. Supported directional, moment, and reconstruction hypotheses informed a prospectively frozen 30-descriptor deployable expansion; four design-only fields were excluded before fresh target access. Formal requalification then used 384 new cases, 96 per scientific family, with zero development-lineage overlap. **(b)** The formal fresh H3 population is the fixed 128-particle-per-case subsample, giving 49,152 samples for neighbour, variance, oracle, family, and coverage diagnostics. **(c)** The directional-frame audit instead covers every particle environment across the 384 fresh cases. Fallback occurred in exactly 515,904 of 627,264 environments, or 82.246710%. This statistic documents frequent degeneracy of the tested observable-defined frame; it does not indicate failure of equivariance, graph neural networks, or alternative frames.

## Figure 6. Fresh operational-identifiability requalification and route conclusion

**(a)** Fresh evidence comprises 384 cases, 49,152 formal samples, five field-lineage-held-out folds, and zero lineage overlap with development evidence. **(b)** Frozen C3/L3 values and preregistered limits for all applicable H3 criteria. P and blue denote a satisfied individual criterion; N and orange denote a criterion not satisfied. Disagreement median and P90 require values no greater than 0.25 and 0.60. Conditional-variance upper 95% requires no greater than 0.35; oracle NRMSE no greater than 0.50; mean-baseline improvement at least 0.20; worst-family NRMSE no greater than 0.75; and coverage at least 0.90. All applicable criteria must pass for a component to qualify. **(c)** Density rate, pressure-gradient acceleration, and viscosity-Laplacian acceleration each failed at least one fresh criterion. Consequently, none satisfied the operational-identifiability qualification, locality was not interpreted, and no neural stage was initiated. The conclusion is limited to the tested deployment-compatible instantaneous observable route.
